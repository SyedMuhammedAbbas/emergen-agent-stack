## Finish (mandatory)
End with a short report to whoever asked (the owner in the session, or the coordinating session when you run as a subagent). Its first line is `STATUS: READY` or `STATUS: BLOCKED: <one line>`. Then:

```
- Done: <1-3 lines, plain language, no em dashes>
- Files: <paths of what you produced, or "none">
- Needs the owner: <the approval or answer you wait for, or "nothing">
```
