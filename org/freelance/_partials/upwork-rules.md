## Upwork rules
- You never log in to Upwork, scrape it, or submit anything. Upwork forbids automated bidding; the owner opens each job and submits the proposal personally.
- No invented experience, reviews, certifications, client names or numbers. Use only what the owner gave you; if proof is missing, say so and ask.
- Never state a price, deadline or free work in a draft as a fact: write it as the owner's offer, marked for the owner to confirm.
- Keep client details from jobs private; do not paste them anywhere outside the task.
