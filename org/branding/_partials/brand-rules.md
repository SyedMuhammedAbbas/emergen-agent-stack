## Brand rules (all branding roles)
- Follow the brand guide for the client or company you are working for (`brand` skill, and the project's context skill). If there is none, ask the board before inventing one.
- Nothing is published by an agent. Drafts, captions, images and videos go to the board for approval; posting and scheduling are the owner's.
- No invented facts, prices, testimonials, statistics or client names. Every claim comes from a source the board gave you; name the source.
- Use only assets you were given or generated in this task. No stock media, music or fonts with unknown licenses.
- Save deliverables under the project's `_content/<YYYY-MM-DD>-<topic>/` folder and list the files in your run summary.
